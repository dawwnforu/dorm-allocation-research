## 从发现到建议

作息与安静需求可转化为入睡时间区间、噪声行为和静音约定等具体提问。开放回答提出的共同主题包括更具体的生活习惯描述、自愿组队、调整空间和公共环境改善。本版只做主题转述，不展示可检索的原话、响应者编号或个人背景。

这支持进一步设计“需求表达—候选推荐—自愿确认—反馈—人工调整”的流程，但尚未检验流程是否改善住宿体验。硬件与制度需求也意味着仅靠推荐算法不足以回应全部问题。

## English interpretation

The table reports counts, denominators and rounded percentages. Q20 lists selected response categories; Q39 lists two selected living needs. Q39 and Q46 allow multiple answers. No joint distributions or respondent identifiers are published.

Recent-experience items have 56 valid answers and different units or recall windows. They should not be compared as a common severity scale or interpreted as clinical diagnoses. The results motivate further design and evaluation; they do not establish an effective allocation intervention.
